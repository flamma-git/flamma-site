01 — Idea

Why we're doing this.

We want to make original animation without waiting for someone else to make it possible.

Flamma Kingdom is our independent animation studio. We are building it around ideas we genuinely care about, starting with our first original series, Memland.

For us, the studio is not only about producing animation. It's about creating the conditions to keep making it.
